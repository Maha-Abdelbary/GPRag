students and graduates to increase their competitive capabilities locally and

❖ Development of faculty members and their assistants to improve the 
performance rate in the learning and teaching methods. 
❖ Set up a special electronic platform to adopt learning methods through 
which the development of students' educational abilities and activating 
modern strategies in teaching and learning can be achieved. 
The second goal: To raise the level of research quality and to confirm the 
excellence of the college in the field of scientific research, through the following 
objectives: 
❖ Implementation of cooperation protocols between the college and the 
corresponding colleges inside and outside the Arab Republic of Egypt. 
❖ Activating research cooperation between faculty members and graduates 
of the various bodies of the health system 
❖ Commitment to the ethics of scientific research and intellectual property 
The third goal: Applying standards of quality and institutional excellence to gain 
the trust of the internal and external community through the following objectives: 
❖ Establishment of the institutional calendar and quality systems 
❖ Supporting the college's internal quality system

Part (1) Definitions and Terms: 
 
Terms 
Definitions 
1- Faculty curricula 
The internal academic regulations (Bachelor's level) for the 
Faculty of Physical therapy with the credit hour system 
2- Study program 
A set of studies and curricula that lead to obtaining an academic 
degree in a specific discipline. 
3- Credited academic 
hour 
It is an academic unit of measurement to determine the weight of 
the course among other educational courses. 
4- Study plan for a 
study program 
List of courses required to be studied for an academic program to 
obtain the required university degree 
5- Academic year 
It consists of three semesters, two main semesters (spring-
autumn) and an optional summer semester. 
Duration of compulsory classes: 15 weeks, excluding exam days. 
The duration of the elective semester is 8 weeks, excluding exam 
days. 
6- Semester average 
A numerical method for evaluating the student academically 
during the semester. 
7- Cumulative 
average 
A numerical method for evaluating the academic performance of a 
student during a specific period. If that period represents the 
completion of the hours required for graduation, then the GPA is 
the GPA for graduation. 
8- Academic load 
The total credit hours registered by the student in one semester. 
9- The 
student's 
academic 
record 
A statement shows the student’s academic progress and includes the 
courses he studied in each semester with their symbols, numbers, 
number of study units, and grades. The record also shows the 
semester average, cumulative average, and general grade statement, 
in addition to the courses from which the transferred student was 
exempted, if this includes all F grades and all improvement grades. 
10- Graduation 
GGPA 
Describe the level of educational attainment of the student during 
his studies at the faculty 
11-Academic advisor He assists the student in planning his study program in 
accordance with his needs and at the same time achieves the 
requirements of the study program. 
12- Academic 
warning 
The notification given to the student because his cumulative 
average is below the limit. the minimum shown in this regulation

➢ The academic year is divided into three semesters as follows: 
- The first main semester (autumn semester): starts in September and 
lasts for 14 weeks. 
- The second main semester (spring semester): starts in February and 
lasts for 14 weeks. 
- Summer semester: starts in the first week of July and lasts for 8 
intensive weeks, equivalent to the contact hours of the course for the 
corresponding ones in the main 
• Important note: The study weeks in the semesters do not include 
the final exams period. 
The first and second semesters: 
- The usual registration for courses begins two weeks before the start of the 
study and ends at the end of the first week of the study, and the study begins 
with the beginning of the second week directly. 
- The student is allowed to modify his registration by adding or deleting 
courses during the first two weeks of each semester and during the third 
week by deletion only. 
Summer semester 
- Registration begins in the last week of June. 
- The first week of the summer semester is devoted to deletion and addition, 
and studies begin during the first week of July. 
- The summer semester courses are determined according to the available 
capabilities and the rules issued by the faculty Council, with the exception 
of clinical training. 
- Joining the summer semester is optional, not compulsory. 
- The minimum number of students to open any course is 20. In cases of 
graduation or expulsion, less than 20 students may open the course after 
the approval of the faculty Council.

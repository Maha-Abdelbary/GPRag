Fundamental Nursing 1 – (Theory) 
This course aims to provide the student with essential knowledge and 
concepts related to the fundamental concepts of health and illness. In 
addition, the student will be provided with all the skills that will 
enable them to perform health assessments for patients with different 
health conditions through utilizing the nursing process to identify 
health alterations, formulate nursing diagnoses, and document 
findings.

• Course Title: Epidemiology 
• Course Code: MED 4263 
• Credit Hours:  2 
Epidemiology  
The course will provide an introduction to the fundamental ideas and 
principles of epidemiology that are relevant to the planning, 
conducting, and interpreting of epidemiological studies. Through this 
course, students should be able to apply and comprehend 
measurements of disease incidence, prevalence, and effect, as well as 
show that they have a basic comprehension of the concepts that 
underpin various study designs. In addition, the students should be 
able to evaluate causality and critically analyze evidence by the end of 
the course. We'll also talk about the implications of using public health 
measurements to predict their potential benefits and the need and 
demand for healthcare.
